# Visuels Entreprise

Trois photos illustratives générées avec le mode built-in image_gen, sans personne réelle ni document d’entreprise. Elles représentent les mises en situation fictives Aster ; elles ne sont pas des témoignages clients. Les captions signalent leur nature illustrative. Les dossiers et alternatives textuelles portent seuls les informations nécessaires aux exercices.

Fichiers consommés par l’application : WebP, largeur 1200 px, optimisation de format avec Sharp uniquement.

- public/enterprise/team-workshop.webp
- public/enterprise/verification-desk.webp
- public/enterprise/pilot-board.webp

## Prompts

### team-workshop

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset for a French workplace learning app, fictional case-study company Aster. Wide editorial photograph, four ordinary adult coworkers of varied ages in a modest small-business meeting room, examining a support workflow together around a table with notebooks and a laptop. Natural candid gestures, realistic office, soft daylight, cream and muted lavender palette. Focus on thoughtful collaborative planning rather than excitement about technology. No legible text, no brand logos, no robots, no holograms, no generated UI charts, no identifiable real people. Landscape composition.

### verification-desk

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Educational illustration photograph for a fictional Aster employee checking an AI-generated customer reply. Wide close oblique view of a realistic desk: hands of an adult comparing two printed documents side by side with a pencil and a laptop blurred in background, a few colored sticky notes to mark claims requiring evidence. Warm daylight, cream paper, muted lavender accents, honest editorial texture. No readable text, no company logos, no personal data, no holograms, no robot. Landscape composition.

### pilot-board

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Educational photograph for fictional small-business Aster AI pilot review. Two adult coworkers in an ordinary meeting room discussing a physical whiteboard with three columns of blank pale sticky notes representing proposed, testing and review stages. One coworker points to a small group of notes while the other records a decision in a notebook. Calm pragmatic scene, natural light, cream and restrained lavender palette, realistic editorial photography. No readable text, logos, fake dashboards, robots, holograms or identifiable real person. Landscape composition.
